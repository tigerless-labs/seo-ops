# C20 · at most one redirect hop (P2)

## Introduction

Chained redirects A→B→C waste crawl budget, lose weight at every hop, and slow users down; 301s stacked across historical redesigns are the easiest way to build up a chain.

## Implementation guidance

- From any inbound URL to the final 200, **at most one hop** (hop ≤1)
- During a redesign migration, **update the old 301 mappings to point straight at the newest URL**; do not stack on top of the previous round's 301:

```
# Anti-pattern: /old → /mid → /new (two hops, historical stacking)
# Correct: at redesign time, change /old's mapping to point straight at /new
/old  301→  /new
/mid  301→  /new
```

- Merge canonicalization (C3) and path migration into a single hop: `http://tigerless.com/old` should reach `https://www.tigerless.com/new` in one hop, not canonicalize first and migrate after
- Internal links write the final URL directly; do not lean on redirects as a fallback (redirects are for external legacy links, not for your own use)
- Self-check: measure redirect chain length while crawling (config `MAX_REDIRECT_HOPS`); periodically replay the historical 301 mapping table
