# T9 · OG share copy + share-image designation (per page)

> Downstream: [C19 social preview cards complete](../../checklist/references/C19.md) — judges the full tag set + `og:image` absolute URL + declared dimensions.

## Introduction

The preview card that pops up when a link is pasted into WeChat, X, Slack, or Feishu is built
entirely from the `og:` tags. If they're missing, the platform scrapes on its own — usually
the first image in the navigation (the logo) and the first text at the top of the page (the
cookie notice).

The difference from [T5 title/description](T5.md) is the **scene**:

- title/desc → search results; the reader is **actively looking**, intent already formed
- OG → the social feed; the reader is **scrolling**, and you have one second to stop them

So the copy may default to the same source, but differentiating is worth it: the search scene
explains what it is; the social scene hooks.

## How to deliver

### Copy

Defaults to title/description; no need to deliver twice. When differentiating, one line each:

```yaml
og_title: A coverage gap during status transition can cost you tens of thousands of dollars
og_description: The gap between F1 and H1B — three scenarios and how insurance bridges each, in one chart.
```

### Share image

**Defaults to the body's lead image**; no delivery needed. Designate one here only when the
lead image doesn't work as a card:

```yaml
og_image: /og/f1-to-h1b-card.png     # designate when the lead image is an icon / wrong size
```

When designation is needed:

- The body has no lead image (a text-only page)
- The lead image is an icon, an avatar, a decorative image (**tigerless.ai is stepping on exactly this today** — its og:image is a 512×512 app icon, so the share renders as a tiny square)
- The lead image is very long (an infographic, a timeline) — cropping to 1200×630 slices the content off; make a dedicated one

Spec: **1200×630 px**, absolute URL, not blocked by robots.

## Common mistakes

- **The logo as the share image** — every share looks identical; readers scroll right past
- **Small text in the image** — the card shrinks tiny on phones and body-size text turns to mush; if the image carries words, make them big — three to five of them
- **A transparent-background PNG** — platforms fill in different backdrops; in dark mode it can become a black blob
- **og_description drier than the desc** — the social scene is the one place a hook is allowed; wasting it is a shame
- **A share image unrelated to the page** — clicking through to a mismatch hurts more than no image

> `og:type` (`article` for article pages) **is not delivered** — the template decides from
> whether the page has an article body; frontend derives it. The same fact is not supplied twice.
