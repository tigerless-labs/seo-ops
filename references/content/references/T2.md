# T2 · key-page list + one-line summary per page (site-level)

> Downstream: [C7 llms.txt exists](../../checklist/references/C7.md) — under-supplied means the file can't be generated (or comes out as a useless whole-site dump).

## Introduction

`llms.txt` is a site guide for AI: one site blurb + one key-page list, each entry with a
one-line summary. It answers "an AI meets this site for the first time — which pages should
it read first?"

The key word is **"key"**. Including the whole site is including nothing — that's the sitemap,
which the AI already has. llms.txt is valuable precisely because **someone made choices**:
which pages best represent what this site can solve. Only someone who knows the content can
make that call, so it's the content team's job; **the file itself is generated by frontend** —
you just deliver the list and the summaries.

## How to deliver

Group by topic, one line each: URL + one sentence.

```markdown
## Products
- /sort/f1 : health insurance options during the F1 student visa, with a school-requirement comparison
- /sort/h1b : H1B work-visa insurance, focused on the gaps outside the employer plan

## Guides
- /guide/f1-to-h1b : how insurance bridges the three status-transition (gap) scenarios
- /guide/claim-process : the full claim process from filing to payout, with common snags

## About
- /aboutus : company credentials, service scope, and the team
```

How to write the summary:

- **<=30 chars: say what problem the page solves**, no marketing copy
- "This page introduces our excellent insurance products" ← zero information; an AI reading it still doesn't know whether to click
- "Health insurance options during the F1 student visa, with a school-requirement comparison" ← a concrete scenario and concrete content
- pSEO page sets are **represented by their hub page**, not listed one by one (stuff hundreds of scenario pages in and the point drowns)

## Common mistakes

- **The summary parrots the title** — then the AI can just read the title; the line adds nothing
- **pSEO listed page by page** — a file thousands of lines long; "key" stops meaning anything
- **New key pages never synced** — this list goes stale; revisit it periodically (suggested: alongside the quarterly content review)
- **Login / business pages included** — those pages shouldn't be indexed in the first place (see [C23](../../checklist/references/C23.md))
