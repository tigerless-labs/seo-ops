# T4 · the `ymyl` verdict (per page)

> Downstream: [C21 YMYL trust block](../../checklist/references/C21.md) — this is the **only switch** for the conditional items.

## Introduction

`ymyl` is a boolean flag deciding whether this page needs the full trust block ([T12](T12.md)):
author real name and credential, reviewer and review date, official-source citations.

**Missing the flag is more dangerous than getting it wrong.** Mark `true` and fall short, and
the C21 human review goes red — someone comes asking. Miss it as `false`, and C21 as a whole
**never triggers** — the report shows not red but "not applicable", so nobody follows up.
**Untested ≠ fine**, but someone reading the report easily takes the two for the same thing.

> **2026-08-25: this entry was originally "page type + ymyl"; the page-type half is retired.**
> Page type once switched the conditional checks on and off, but Google doesn't consume
> `WebPage` subtypes (`ItemPage` / `CollectionPage` / `AboutPage` etc.), and maintaining
> page-type declarations means content judging every page, failing silently when missed —
> an input that needs daily human feeding with no downstream consumer isn't worth keeping.
> Conditional items now trigger on the flag and content conditions.

## How to deliver

One line at the top of the page doc:

```yaml
ymyl: true
```

Criterion: **would this page's content affect the reader's health, finances, legal standing,
or physical safety?**

| Page | ymyl | Why |
|---|---|---|
| insurance product page | ✓ | buying wrong directly hits finances and access to care |
| claim-process guide | ✓ | doing it wrong means no payout |
| status-transition insurance bridging | ✓ | legal status + finances |
| company intro / team page | — | misreading it costs nothing real |
| brand story | — | same |

**When unsure, mark `true`.** The costs are asymmetric: a mistaken `true` means one extra
trust block; a mistaken `false` means YMYL content shipping naked — that's the R2 red line.

## Common mistakes

- **Judging only "is it medical"** — finances, law, and physical safety count the same; insurance trips nearly all of it
- **Forgetting on a revision** — a brand-story page later gains claim-filing steps and `ymyl` doesn't follow
- **`false` delivered while the page carries author and reviewer bylines** — then it's actually YMYL content and the flag contradicts the content; C21 doesn't trigger, so nobody verifies whether the bylines are real (worse than no bylines at all)
- **Blanket `true` across the site** — looks safe, but actually turns T12 into a per-page mandatory item; when content can't keep up they start inventing; **an invented review byline is the R2 red line**, worse than a missed flag
