# T13 · product fields (conditional: triggered by product pages)

> Trigger: **the page shows a product with pricing** (human-written product pages and pSEO bulk scenario pages treated alike)
> · Downstream: [C12 JSON-LD complete and valid](../../checklist/references/C12.md)

## Introduction

A product page's JSON-LD (`InsuranceProduct` / `Product` / `Offer`) is not conjured from
nothing; every field needs a human-readable source. This item supplies that source.

The key is the **form of delivery**: one field per slot, registered structured.
Don't write a paragraph of introduction for the engineer to dig numbers out of — dig wrong
and C12 goes red, and next time the price changes nobody knows which paragraph to fix.

## How to deliver

```yaml
product:
  name: F1 Student Health Insurance · Standard Plan
  description: Covers outpatient, hospitalization, prescriptions, and repatriation; satisfies most university waiver requirements.
  price: 89
  price_currency: USD
  price_period: month               # if there is a billing period it must be stated, or readers take it for a total
  coverage_limit: 500000            # annual limit, USD
  deductible: 250
  provider: Tigerless Insurance Services, Inc.
  valid_from: 2026-01-01
  valid_through: 2026-12-31
```

Key points:

- **Write `price` only when there is a price.** If the price is "contact us" / "it depends",
  **don't deliver the field** — an Offer missing `price`/`priceCurrency` is a straight red in
  C12; better to declare no Offer than one you can't fill
- Numbers are just numbers, no currency symbols or units (units have their own fields)
- Every field's value must be **visible on the page** — $89 in the JSON-LD while the page
  says "from $79" is markup inconsistent with visible content; the C21/C12 human review goes red

## Common mistakes

- **Delivering a paragraph** — "Our standard plan starts at $89 a month, with an annual
  coverage limit of five hundred thousand": the engineer must dig three numbers out of prose,
  and digging one wrong is inevitable
- **Invented ratings** — delivering "user rating 4.8/5" and expecting it to enter the JSON-LD.
  **`AggregateRating` is banned site-wide**; the checker scans for it negatively and goes
  straight red. The reason: Google's structured-data policy forbids self-serving reviews —
  this isn't us being conservative, it's a manual-action offense. Show testimonials if you
  like, but **they don't enter structured data**
- **A "from" price** — `price` is one definite number; ranges use `lowPrice`/`highPrice` (needs a separate agreement)
- **Coverage limit and deductible buried in the description** — the two fields readers compare most; only as standalone fields can they be cited
- **Expired plans left up** — `valid_through` has passed and it's still hanging there: that's wrong information
